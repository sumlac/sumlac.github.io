MY THINKING / CONTINUOUS OFFENSE + DEFENSE

Two factories.
One tighter loop.

I want security to learn as fast as the systems it protects. My thesis is two connected factories: one constantly challenges the system; the other turns what we learn into controls we can prove. Shared evidence makes the next cycle better.

01 / DEFENSE

Defense Factory

My vision for turning a reproducible failure into a verified control. Repair the boundary, prove legitimate work still succeeds, and keep the test that catches a recurrence.

REPRODUCE→REPAIR→VERIFY

Offense Factory

My vision for constant red teaming. Challenge models, retrieval, tools, and permissions as they change. Turn each meaningful failure into evidence the defender can use.

THE FEEDBACK LOOP IS THE PRODUCT

I care about the time from discovery to a verified fix, the cost of each cycle, and whether the same failure can happen again. The pages explore how I would build that loop.
